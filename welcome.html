What SmartRecSys does

Personalised to you

Recommendations are built from your department, semester, completed courses, and stated interests — not a one-size-fits-all list.

Ranked, not just listed

Every result carries a transparent match score so you can see exactly why a course ranked where it did.

Fast suggestions

Submit your profile once and get a ranked list of relevant courses in seconds — no manual browsing through catalogues.

Domain-aware scoring

The model weighs domain relevance and difficulty alignment together, producing a single calibrated score per course.

Smart academic planning

Understand which electives complement your existing coursework and align with your career goals before you register.

Easy-to-use interface

A clean, accessible form collects your profile in under two minutes. No account creation or login required.

Why use SmartRecSys?

  • Saves time — no more manually cross-referencing syllabi to find relevant electives.
  • Better academic planning — see how each course fits your current semester and degree path.
  • Transparent reasoning — every recommendation includes a plain-language explanation of why it was suggested.
  • Helps choose relevant electives — filter by domain and difficulty to narrow down exactly what you need.
  • Machine learning backbone — scores are computed by a trained model, not hand-curated rules.
