About SmartRecSys

An academic course recommendation system built as a final-year major project.

Purpose

Students in technical degree programmes often face a large catalogue of elective courses with little guidance on which ones align with their existing knowledge, current semester workload, or career direction. SmartRecSys addresses this by collecting a lightweight academic profile and running it through a trained machine learning model to produce a ranked, explained list of relevant courses.

Problem being solved

Manual course selection is time-consuming and often based on word-of-mouth rather than data. Students may miss courses that would significantly strengthen their profile, or enrol in courses that duplicate knowledge they already have. SmartRecSys makes the selection process faster, more transparent, and better aligned with individual goals.

How the AI works

The recommendation engine takes your department, semester, completed courses, preferred domains, and difficulty preference as input. It computes a relevance score for each course in the catalogue by combining domain alignment, difficulty fit, and prerequisite coverage into a single weighted value. Courses are then ranked by this score and returned with a plain-language reason for each recommendation.

Intended users

Undergraduate and postgraduate students in computer science, information technology, software engineering, and related disciplines who want data-driven guidance when choosing elective or specialisation courses.

Key Features

Personalised Recommendations

Results are specific to your profile — not a generic top-10 list.

Machine Learning Model

Scores are computed by a trained model, not hand-written rules.

Easy Navigation

A single form, a single results page — no complex multi-step flows.

Smart Academic Planning

Understand how each course fits your degree path before you register.

Responsive Design

Works cleanly on desktop, tablet, and mobile without a separate mobile build.
